# Saints Tickets

A marketplace app where verified University of St Andrews students buy and sell tickets to Students' Union events from each other.

- Verified students only (uni email + phone)
- Buyers pay through the app; the money is held until the ticket arrives in their Union ticket account, then paid out to the seller
- Missed transfer = automatic full refund

Payments by Stripe. iOS and Android app in development.
